Why I built this

Digital design shouldn't need
a 20 GB install and a $10,000 license.

I kept watching people who wanted to learn Verilog hit the same wall: the tool needed to see their logic was harder to install than the logic itself. So I built RTL Bench — a browser-based EDA workbench that compiles, simulates, and times real RTL against open-source process libraries, with zero setup.

It runs on Yosys, Icarus Verilog, and Eclipse Layout Kernel — the same open tools professional flows are built on — and it runs entirely on free-tier infrastructure. If you can open a URL, you can design a chip.

— Raghavan, creator of RTL Bench

Engineered for real RTL workflows

Not a diagram toy — every feature maps to a step in an actual synthesis and timing-signoff pipeline.

📁

Multi-file SystemVerilog & VHDL projects

Drop a folder of .sv, .v, or .vhd sources and data files; relative paths are preserved so $readmemh finds its files. Top modules and entities are auto-detected.

🧩

Hierarchy-preserving synthesis

Submodule instances render as encapsulated blocks with named port pins and input/output direction dots. Toggle Hierarchy to collapse into blocks, or Flat to unroll every gate — from the same compile.

⏱

Gate-level static timing analysis

Yosys techmap decomposes the design to real gates, then the longest structural path is measured as gate depth. Per-process-node conversion (180/90/45/7 nm) gives a nanosecond estimate with top-K critical paths and a cell-by-cell breakdown.

🔍

Bidirectional cross-probing

Double-click any identifier in the code to highlight its gates on the schematic and dim non-target waveform traces to 18% opacity. Inspect data flows from source line to digital waveform seamlessly.

🛡️

Structural Clock Domain Crossing (CDC)

Topological back-tracing inspects every register clock boundary. Flags combinational hazards and missing synchronizers while verifying multi-stage flip-flop synchronizer chains. Full report page with a domain interaction matrix and per-path diagnostics.

🔁

Reset Domain Crossing (RDC) analysis

Structural RDC detection across reset boundaries — catches the same-clock / different-reset bugs that CDC tools miss. Traces reset nets back to their source, verifies 2-FF reset synchronizer chains, and flags combinational reset generators and soft resets before tape-out.

⚡

FPGA synthesis & WebSerial flashing

Go from RTL to physical silicon without terminal tools. Compiles with Yosys and nextpnr, auto-recovers from pin conflicts, and streams bitstreams directly to Tang Nano and iCE40 boards via WebSerial.

⚡

FPGA bitstream generation

Take your design from RTL to physical silicon. One click compiles, places, routes, and packs a bitstream for Gowin Tang Nano 9K, Tang Nano 20K, and Lattice iCE40 UP5K boards — or download the binary and flash it yourself.

📈

Bring your own testbench — or auto-generate one

Drop tb_yourdesign.sv next to your design and RTL Bench auto-detects it by filename or $dumpvars signature. The testbench is excluded from synthesis, STA, and FPGA builds but runs in simulation. No TB? It reads your top module's ports with Yosys and generates one: reset release, 60 random stimulus cycles, drain. Waveforms appear in the built-in drawer either way.

🧵

Vector bus collapsing

Bit-blasted Yosys nets are regrouped into single vector wires with IEEE /W slash notation and a width label. Branch fan-out is drawn with proper solder dots, so wide datapaths stay legible.

🔗

Fused constant comparators

An == against a literal like 8'hA5 is rendered as a single comparator cell with the value fused into an adjacent chip. No dangling constant wires — the operation reads as one unit.

🖱️

Click-to-trace nets

Every wire is interactive. Click any net to highlight the full routed path across the canvas — from a primary input through combinational logic to a register or output pin. Ideal for tracking feedback loops.

RTL to waveform, in five stages

The pipeline below runs on every compile and simulate — the same flow your commercial toolchain uses.

1

Upload or paste your RTL

Drop a folder of sources, paste a single file, or pick a preset. Top module and data files are auto-detected.

2

Yosys RTLIL elaboration

Your source is sandboxed and elaborated by read_verilog → proc → opt → clean. Clocks and resets are inferred automatically.

3

ELK placement & routing

Eclipse Layout Kernel positions cells in a LEFT→RIGHT layered graph and routes every net with orthogonal Manhattan edges.

4

Inspect & trace

Zoom through the hierarchy, click nets to trace them, and export the current view as a clean vector SVG.

5

Simulate & inspect

Run an auto-generated testbench, watch signals toggle in the waveform drawer, and verify behaviour before sign-off.
